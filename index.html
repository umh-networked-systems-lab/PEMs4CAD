Perception Error Models: interactive visualizer

Choose a LiDAR, a perception stage and the target's position, then draw samples from the PEMs.

Overview (ego-vehicle at the origin, x forward, y left)
Zoom on the target: ground truth (grey) vs. estimate (orange, dashed)

JavaScript port of the Matlab/Python PEMs. Position and heading errors are sampled as absolute values with a random sign; the existence probability is capped at 1. Distances are limited to each configuration's domain. Where a model diverges near the end of its range, errors can become very large.
